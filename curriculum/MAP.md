# Curriculum map: one year

The plan the track authors write against. Lesson files are the truth; this map fixes
**ids, titles and order** so cross-track prerequisites line up before the lessons exist.
Course week 1 = 2026-11-09 (ISO 2026-W46). Content ships in **waves of ~6 weeks**.

## Pace

The schedule gives one focus part per weekday (Mon T, Tue E/K, Wed V, Thu T, Fri E/K,
Sat V + C/L, Sun test + project), 25–50 min each. Lessons are 10–15 min. Days where a
track runs out of new content are topped up with **practice drills** from passed lessons,
so the map aims for this many new lessons per week, not more:

| Track | New lessons / week | Year total (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| T theory | 3 | ~150 |
| E ear | 2 | ~100 |
| K keys | 2 | ~100 |
| V voice | 3 (Wed 2, Sat 1) | ~150 |
| C composition | 1 | ~50 |
| L lyrics | 1 | ~50 |
| Projects | 1 (Sun) | 52, none repeated within the year (13 per kind) |

**Voice safety (SPEC, enforced by validation):** every V lesson starts with a check-in and
a warm-up. Distortion lessons are tagged `distortion` and must (transitively) require a
lesson tagged `support` (V-002) and one tagged `onset` (V-005), state the stop protocol in
both languages, and are capped at one per day by the planner. Nothing ever scores more
distortion higher. Register/technique labels from voice-score are hints, never grades.

## Wave 2 lessons (weeks 9–14)

Same rules as wave 1. Four-part (SATB) work: the music21 worker checks at most two
voices, so four-part writing is taught with `midi-play` (exact voicings, `octave: exact`),
`mcq` (spot the error) and two-voice `compose` (outer voices). The `progression`
generator has `smooth: true` (voice-led, no parallels) for playing progressions.
